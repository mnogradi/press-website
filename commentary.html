Memorial Dedication

In Memory of Bill W. Lee, Sr.

(1947-2026) — Founder & Publisher, The Baptist Standard Bearer

This series is dedicated to the memory and legacy of Bill W. Lee. His decades of untiring labor to preserve historical Baptist literature and Biblical orthodoxy shaped both our faith and the founding vision of North Pass Press. May these freshly restored pages continue the work he loved.
The Design Manifesto

Why This Edition Exists

This edition is designed to be highly readable and comfortable to use, with larger font sizes, more space, and a refined layout for serious and prolonged study.

I

Single-Column Typography

Replacing cramped, multi-column grids with a spacious, single-column larger print reading layout designed for continuous study and legibility.

II

Modern Standards

Retypeset and printed on high-quality cream paper with generous margins and helpful navigation through the table of contents and indexes.

III

Faithful Unabridged Text

Preserving John Gill's original cross-references and verse-by-verse exposition in full, without modern truncation or editing.
