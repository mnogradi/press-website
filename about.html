Our Mission

To the Modern Reader

North Pass Press is an independent publishing house primarily dedicated to the restoration of historical texts. We believe that the voices of the English and Reformed traditions are not mere relics, but living works intended for the modern library.

Our work focuses on the recovery of forgotten, lost, and - perhaps - even ignored historical texts, including everything from the writings of the 17th century to the expansive breadth of English poetry. By providing editions that are newly re-typeset and unabridged, we aim to respect both the integrity of the source and the needs of the modern reader.

Our mission is to provide bibliophiles of all ages with meticulously curated editions that honor both content and craftsmanship, bridging the foundations of heritage with the standards of modern typography.

Historical Restoration

The Press specializes in the recovery of historical literature. All of our publications are given careful and detailed consideration, as we strive to accurately preserve the original text.

Typographic Design

The Press is dedicated to ensuring that every volume is clear, intentional, and faithful to its source. We utilize professional typesetting and manual layout to create editions that are both beautiful and accessible.

Primary Sources

We are committed to maintaining the integrity of the primary sources. These texts are chosen with care, and are intended to provide a direct connection to the forgotten works of the past.
