What We Deliver

Print & Digital Production

Our primary focus is what goes between the covers. By concentrating on interior typesetting and standard digital formatting, we ensure your text receives meticulous microtypographic care.

Print Edition

Print-Ready PDFs

Engineered to exact printer preflight specifications for Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Lulu, Bookvault, or short-run offset presses.

  • • Precision trim sizes (5×8, 5.5×8.5, 6×9, custom)
  • • Custom margins, gutter allowance, & bleeds
  • • Running headers, folios, & chapter architecture
  • • Complete frontmatter & backmatter formatting
Digital Edition

Standard EPUB Ebooks

Clean, standards-compliant reflowable .epub files that render consistently across all standard e-readers and retail platforms.

  • • Universal standard EPUB format
  • • Active, clickable navigation & Table of Contents
  • • Clean semantic hierarchy & clear typography
  • • Compatible with KDP, Apple Books, Kobo, & ereaders
Typography

Typst-Powered Precision

We leverage modern typesetting technology to produce book interiors with the typographic rigor of traditional fine presses.

  • • Advanced hyphenation & justification algorithms
  • • Elimination of widows, orphans, & runts
  • • True small caps, oldstyle figures, & ligatures
  • • Timeless book faces: Garamond, Caslon, & Junicode
Specialization

How It Works

Our Typesetting Process

From raw manuscript to finished publication files, we work closely with you through every stage.

01

Manuscript Review

We review your manuscript, discuss target trim sizes and printers (KDP, IngramSpark, Lulu, Bookvault), and confirm whether you need print, standard EPUB, or both.

02

Sample Style Proof

We typeset an opening chapter showing font pairings, chapter heads, running headers, and drop caps for your feedback and approval.

03

Full Book Typesetting

Once the style is approved, we typeset the entire book, meticulously inspecting every page spread to resolve widows, orphans, and awkward breaks.

04

Proofing & Delivery

You review the complete proofs. We make final revisions and deliver pristine, preflight-checked print PDFs and validated standard EPUB files.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you design book covers?

Our primary focus is interior typesetting. However, if you like our aesthetic, cover design can be included upon request. Just let us know when you reach out.

Printers & Formats

We produce print-ready PDFs formatted for Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Lulu, Bookvault, or offset printers, as well as standard reflowable EPUB ebooks. We accept manuscripts in Word (.docx), Markdown, or plain text.

Quotes & Pricing

Every manuscript is unique. We provide individual quotes based on word count, structural complexity (prose vs. verse, dialogue, or footnotes), and whether you require print, EPUB, or both.
