Return Policy

 · 

1. Returns & Refunds

North Pass Press takes care in preparing and fulfilling every order. If something is not right with your purchase, please contact us and we will be glad to help.


2. Printed Books

We accept returns of printed books within 30 days of delivery.

To qualify for a return, books must be unused, unread, and in their original condition. Customers are responsible for return shipping costs for returns due to a change of mind.

Please contact North Pass Press before sending a return. Once the returned item has been received and inspected, a refund will be issued to the original payment method within 10 business days.


3. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Orders

If your book arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, please contact us within 30 days of delivery.

Please include your order number and, where applicable, a photograph of the issue. We will arrange an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement or a full refund. North Pass Press will cover any return shipping costs associated with an incorrect or defective order.

4. Digital Publications

Due to the nature of digital products, purchases of downloadable publications are generally non-refundable once the files have been downloaded, except where required by applicable law.

If you experience a problem accessing or downloading a purchase, receive an incorrect file, or are charged more than once, please contact us so that we can resolve the issue.

5. Orders Placed Through Other Retailers

Orders placed through third-party retailers or marketplaces are subject to the return and refund policies of the retailer through which the purchase was made. Please contact that retailer directly for assistance.

6. Contact

For questions about returns, refunds, or an order, please contact us at contact@northpass.press.

Nothing in this policy affects any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection laws.
