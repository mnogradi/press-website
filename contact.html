Common Inquiries

General FAQ

What does the Press publish?

Our primary work is the restoration of historical literature and theology. However, the catalog is open to modern moral fiction and theology that align with our standards for quality and theological integrity.

What titles are planned next?

We currently have several titles planned across various genres, and are always on the lookout for new books to publish. Each edition is built from the ground up with meticulous care.

Release Schedule

Because our editions are prepared individually, release times vary per book. Factors include book length, along with the quality and availability of source materials.

Restoration Standards

Each edition is built with careful attention to readability, typography, and accuracy. Preview pages on each book listing allow readers to judge the quality for themselves.
