General FAQ

What does the Press publish?

Our primary work is the restoration of historical literature and theology. However, the catalog is open to modern moral fiction and theology that align with our standards for quality and theological integrity.

What titles are planned next?

We currently have several titles planned across various genres, and are always on the lookout for new books to publish.

How many books are released each year?

Because our editions are prepared individually, release times vary per book. Factors that influence release times include book length, along with the quality and availability of source materials.

What is the standard for restoration?

Each edition is built from the ground up, with careful attention to readability, typography, and accuracy. Preview pages on each book listing allow readers to judge the quality for themselves.
